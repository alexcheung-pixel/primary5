HK Primary 5 Grammar Unit 2

Describing People: "WITH" vs "IN"

Learn when to use WITH and IN to describe someone's features, clothes, and accessories!

Rule #1
WITH

Use "WITH" for...

Body Parts, Facial Features & Accessories

👤 Body Parts & Features:
  • a round face, a long face
  • curly blonde hair, long straight hair
  • big brown eyes, dimples, freckles
👓 Accessories / Carried Items:
  • glasses, sunglasses
  • a bow tie
  • an umbrella, a backpack

💡 Pattern Example:

• "Who's the boy with the long face?"

• "Who's the girl with the glasses?"

Rule #2
IN

Use "IN" for...

Clothing Items & Worn Garments

🧥 Clothing & Clothes Items:
  • a suit, a vest, a jacket, a raincoat
  • gloves, mittens
  • sandals, boots, shoes
  • a red dress, a school uniform

💡 Pattern Example:

• "Who's the girl in the vest?"

• "Who's the man in a dark suit?"
