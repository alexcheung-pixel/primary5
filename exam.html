Hong Kong GE 5 Syllabus

Master Your P5 Grammar!

Review the essential grammar topics from Units 1 to 4. Each category features core rules, real Hong Kong everyday examples, and key test traps to watch out for in your exams!

Unit 1 & 2 Focus
01

Modal Verbs & Good Manners

Rules for should / shouldn't and friendship etiquette.

Key Structure:

• should / shouldn't + base verb (e.g., We should offer our seats.)

• should / shouldn't + be + adjective (e.g., We shouldn't be jealous of others.)

Collocations & Prepositions:

• listen to | argue / quarrel with | say sorry to

• take care of | be jealous of | be nice to

Unit 1 & 2 Focus
02

Adverbs of Manner & Feelings

Describing actions, degrees of feelings, and irregular adverbs.

Regular vs. Irregular Rules:

• Most add -ly: soft → softly, polite → politely

• Ending in -y: noisy → noisily, messy → messily

• Irregular Trap! early → early, late → late, fast → fast, good → well

Degree Modifiers + Feelings:

• so / very / really / a bit / a little + annoyed / upset / embarrassed / shocked

Unit 3 Focus
03

Descriptions & Relative Pronouns

Using with, in, and relative clause pronoun who.

Appearance & Clothing:

• with + body parts / accessories: the boy with glasses / curly hair / round face

• in + clothing items: the girl in the red dress / gloves / suit

Relative Pronoun 'who':

• Connects person to an action: Pam is the girl who is waving at the audience.

Unit 3 & 4 Focus
04

Prepositions & Possessives

Time/Place prepositions and possessive pronouns vs. Whose.

Prepositions (at / in / on):

• Time: at 10 a.m. / night / Christmas | in July / winter / 2026 | on Monday / 2nd Oct / Christmas Day

• Place: at the bus stop / entrance | in bed / class / Sha Tin | on the 18th floor / Lantau Island

Possessives & Question Words:

• my → mine, your → yours, her → hers, their → theirs

• Whose (ownership) vs. Who's (Who is)
